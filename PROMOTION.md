## The post

**"I built the writing tool I always wanted — so I'm giving it away."**

For months, while writing my own books, I kept hitting the same wall: AI helps me draft, but it **dilutes my voice**, fumbles Greek, and quietly forgets what happened three chapters ago.

So I built the fix — and it turned into a small obsession.

🪨 **Caveman Writer Studio** is a **single HTML file** — no install, no account, works offline — that turns a spark of an idea (or an unfinished story) into a **complete novel**, in **your** voice:

📖 **Engineered briefs** for novels, novellas and serials — with 19 master techniques baked in: King, Kazantzakis, Tolkien, Lovecraft, Christie, Dostoevsky, García Márquez, Hemingway, Orwell, Murakami, Le Guin, Woolf, G.R.R. Martin, Austen, Camus, le Carré, Vizyinos, Papadiamantis, Herbert.

🧠 **An author profile that matches YOU** — paste a sample of your prose, and the AI writes like you. Built-in **Greek language guard** (flawless orthography and tonics) + a **proofreading gate** with an independent-reviewer pass and a human checklist.

📚 **A manuscript tab that actually tracks the book** — chapters, live word counts (% of the book), a weekly-rhythm planner, and even a token/cost tracker.

📖 **Seed-to-novel extender** — paste an unfinished story; get a canon-preserving novel plan with 3-act structure and a per-chapter word budget.

⚡ And the namesake trick: the **caveman protocol** cuts ~65% of the tokens the AI spends on commentary — full substance, zero fluff.

**Why free?** Because a good writing tool should belong to writers, not to a subscription. MIT licensed, built by an author, for authors.
