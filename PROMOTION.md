## 2 · First release — Release notes (v1.0.0)

> **Caveman Writer Studio — v14.0 (first public release)**
>
> A complete, single-file writing studio for authors who write with AI but want to keep their voice, their language, and their money.
>
> 🪨 **What's inside**
> - Generate engineered briefs for novels, novellas and serials (chapter-cliffhanger mode)
> - 19 master techniques (King · Kazantzakis · Tolkien · Lovecraft · Christie · Dostoevsky · García Márquez · Hemingway · Orwell · Murakami · Le Guin · Woolf · G.R.R. Martin · Austen · Camus · le Carré · Vizyinos · Papadiamantis · Herbert)
> - 🧠 Author profile with house style distilled from real published prose + voice-sample matching
> - Greek language guard + proofreading gate + independent reviewer with human checklist
> - 🎲 Spark, 🧬 character generator, 🎬 beat sheets, 🎭 reader-sim, 🔗 promises/payoffs
> - 📖 Seed-to-novel extender: paste any unfinished story → canon-preserving novel with 3-act structure & per-chapter word budget
> - 📚 Manuscript tab: chapters, live word counts, weekly rhythm planner, cost tracker
> - Caveman protocol: ~65% fewer tokens in AI commentary, full substance kept
> - Bilingual EN/ΕΛ · 3 themes · compact & focus modes · autosave · export/import
>
> 📄 Single HTML file — no install, no dependencies, works offline.
>
> Attribution: builds on JuliusBrussee/caveman (MIT) and ideas from haowjy/creative-writing-skills (Apache-2.0). MIT licensed.
